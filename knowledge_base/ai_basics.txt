Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science focused on creating systems that can perform tasks that typically require human intelligence.

Common AI capabilities include:
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Computer Vision
- Speech Recognition
- Recommendation Systems
- Decision Making

Machine Learning is a subset of AI that allows systems to learn patterns from data.

Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses neural networks with multiple layers.

Popular AI frameworks include:
- TensorFlow
- PyTorch
- Scikit-Learn

AI applications include:
- Chatbots
- Autonomous vehicles
- Fraud detection
- Medical diagnosis
- Cybersecurity threat detection

Python is the most widely used programming language for AI development because of its extensive ecosystem of libraries and community support.

Large Language Models (LLMs) are AI models trained on massive text datasets to generate and understand human language.
