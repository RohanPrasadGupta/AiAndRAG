TechNova Internal Company Policies

Working Hours:
Employees are expected to work between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM local time.

Remote Work:
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.

Password Policy:
- Minimum length: 12 characters
- Must contain uppercase letters
- Must contain lowercase letters
- Must contain numbers
- Must contain special characters

Multi-Factor Authentication:
MFA is mandatory for all employees.

Data Classification:

Public:
Information that can be shared externally.

Internal:
Information intended for employees only.

Confidential:
Sensitive business information.

Restricted:
Highly sensitive information requiring strict access controls.

Leave Policy:
Employees receive 20 days of annual leave per year.

Security Policy:
Employees must report suspected security incidents within one hour of discovery.

Use of personal USB devices on company systems is prohibited.
